# ARENA360 DEFEND (stub)

DEFEND is a product module in ARENA360. The hosted MCP at `https://arena-mcp.lsports.eu/arena/mcp` exposes **zero** DEFEND tools as of 2026-09-27.

## What to tell the operator

DEFEND risk controls are not available through this connection yet. I cannot read or change liability, limits, or bettor-risk settings from here.

Point them to support@lsports.eu and to the ARENA360 DEFEND screens in the platform.

Access stays read-only for this module until tools exist.

## What not to do

- Do not invent `defend_*` or any other risk-tool name.
- Do not map DEFEND questions onto `ordering_*`, `configuration_*`, `boost_*`, `coveragehub_*`, or `tradingfloor_*`.
- Do not request end-bettor PII or betting history as a workaround.
- Do not treat BOOST margin or TRADE configuration as DEFEND.
